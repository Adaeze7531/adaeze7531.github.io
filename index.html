A little about my work

Support that sees the bigger picture.

Administrative support is not only about completing a task. It is also about understanding what the task connects to, who needs the information, and what needs to happen next.

My work has developed through hands-on responsibility, not just training. At Womenful Voice, I progressed from volunteering into Volunteer Resources support and management, taking on responsibilities that connect people, processes and information.

This work has involved coordinating recruitment and interviews, supporting volunteer onboarding, maintaining records, organizing access to shared resources, communicating with team members, and helping coordinate virtual events and engagement activities. Each process involves more than one task; it requires the right information, clear handoffs and follow-through.

I bring that same operational perspective to administrative support. I want to understand how a task fits into the wider process, what other people need from it, and what must happen next. That helps me support not only the immediate request, but also the coordination around it.

My approach is practical: keep records useful, make information easier to locate, document recurring work where needed, and communicate clearly with the people involved. I also recognize when a decision belongs with a department lead rather than assuming responsibility for decisions outside my role.

I bring a people-aware, process-minded approach to operations: connecting the details, supporting the people involved, and helping work move from request to completion.

My working approach

Understand the process. Connect the pieces.

I aim to make work easier to follow, not add unnecessary complexity. My approach begins with understanding how the team already works and where support is needed.

01

Understand the context

02

Organize and coordinate

03

Follow through and communicate

Keep relevant people informed, surface pending items and escalate decisions when they require a manager's input.
