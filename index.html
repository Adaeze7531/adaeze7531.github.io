The work behind the work

Small things become big distractions when nobody has time to manage them.

An unanswered email may only take a few minutes. A misplaced document may seem insignificant. One missed follow-up may not feel like much. But when these things happen repeatedly, they create friction across the whole team.

About Adaeze

I don't just take tasks off a list. I look for the friction behind them.

I'm Adaeze Obi, a Virtual Assistant specializing in administrative and operational support. I work across the everyday details that keep a team functioning: communication, documentation, digital organization, coordination, follow-ups, records, and recurring processes.

My experience has taught me that administrative work is rarely just one task. An onboarding process might involve communication, forms, access, folders, groups and documentation. An event might involve an agenda, speakers, reminders, presentation materials, communication and post-event follow-up.

I enjoy taking those moving pieces and giving them structure. That can mean organizing a shared Drive, creating a practical workflow, keeping information accessible to the right people, supporting an inbox, coordinating a process, or documenting what needs to happen next.

I also pay attention to what makes a process harder than it needs to be. If information keeps getting lost, a folder structure may need attention. If a recurring task keeps being forgotten, it may need a documented process or clearer ownership.

The goal is not simply to keep busy work moving. It's to create enough structure that the people I support can spend more time on the work only they can do.
